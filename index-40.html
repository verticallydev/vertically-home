Questions

Questions roofers ask us

Something else on your mind? Call or text Savannah at (360) 623-5598, any time.

Do I keep my business phone number?

Yes. Your number stays with your phone company. Calls you don't answer forward to Savannah, and she can send emergencies straight back to your cell.

Are Savannah, Madison and Austin real people?

No. They're AI, and they say so if anyone asks. Callers can ask for a person at any time, and Savannah connects them to you or to our live team.

What does my team do without asking me?

Everyday work, like follow-up texts, review replies and website updates, happens on its own and shows up in your Monday report. Anything big, like raising an ad budget or texting a whole neighborhood, waits for your OK in the app.

How does the 3-inspection guarantee work?

Keep your missed calls forwarding to Savannah for your first 60 days. If she books you fewer than 3 inspections in that time, your next month is free. No forms, we check it from your CRM.

What does one roofer per town mean?

We only work with one roofer in each town, because Madison can't run ads and content for two roofers competing for the same homeowners. Once a roofer in your town joins, it's closed to others.

Does it work with QuickBooks?

Yes. Invoices and payments sync to QuickBooks Online, so your books stay up to date without double entry.

I already use JobNimbus, AccuLynx or Roofr. Do I have to switch?

You can switch any time, and we move your data for free. If you're in the middle of a contract, we can send your leads into your current CRM until it ends.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel online in one click. Your old website comes back, calls go straight to your phone, and you can export all of your data.
