Questions roofers ask us

Something else? Call or text Savannah at (360) 623-5598, any time.

Do I keep my business phone number?

Yes. Your number stays with your phone company. Calls you don't answer forward to Savannah, and she can send emergencies straight back to your cell.

Are Savannah, Madison and Austin real people?

No. They're AI, and they say so if anyone asks. Callers can ask for a person at any time, and Savannah connects them to you or our live team.

What does my team do without asking me?

Everyday work, like follow-ups, review replies and website updates, happens on its own and shows up in your Monday report. Anything big waits for your OK in the app.

I use JobNimbus, AccuLynx or Roofr. Do I have to switch?

You can switch any time, and we move your data for free. If you're in a contract, we can send your leads into your current CRM until it ends.

How does the 3-inspection guarantee work?

Keep your missed calls forwarding to Savannah for 60 days. If she books fewer than 3 inspections, your next month is free. We check it from your CRM.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel online in one click. Your old website comes back, calls go straight to your phone, and you can export all of your data.
